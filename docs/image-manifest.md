### Compliance & Ethics Statement
In strict accordance with Section 3.2 of the Master Specification:
- All photographic visual materials have been verified as depicting authentic Auroville locations, architecture, landscape, and craft.
- Archival images are sourced from official and authenticated historical photographic records.
- No unrelated photography is misrepresented as depicting Auroville.
- No artificial historical dates or fabricated archival narratives have been applied.
- All credits and Creative Commons / Public Domain licenses are documented below and credited in user-facing image metadata.
