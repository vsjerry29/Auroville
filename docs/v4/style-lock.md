# Auroville V4 Style Lock & Art Direction Bible

## 1. The Core Metaphor
> *A single hand-drawn mark escapes the page, grows into an illustrated living world, and invites each visitor to leave a mark of their own.*

The website is an **illustrated living editorial publication**. All visual elements exist as if crafted on heavy handmade archival paper with genuine physical art media.

## 3. Four Illustration Treatments, One Artistic Hand

1. **Graphite Architecture (Chapters 01, 03, 09):**
   - Perspective guidelines, radial drafting grids, compass circles.
   - Clean structural silhouettes with layered 45° and 60° hatching.
   - Restrained touches of warm gold leaf on acoustic disc arrays.
2. **Botanical Color Studies (Chapters 04, 05, 06, 10):**
   - Fine-point ink outlines with selective watercolor / colored-pencil washes.
   - Individual branch anatomies, aerial roots, neem leaves, and tropical dry evergreen flora.
3. **Human-Story Ink (Chapters 07, 08):**
   - Expressive anatomy and gestural drafting of artisans, builders, and community makers.
   - Warm earthy washes depicting terracotta clay shaping, architectural blueprint analysis, and tree planting.
4. **Paper-Cut Conceptual Collage (Chapters 02, 11, 12):**
   - Layered paper relief with subtle drop contours.
   - Crisp cut-outs revealing panoramic vistas through folding apertures.

## 4. Typography Scale & Composition Hierarchy

- **Monumental Display:** *Archivo Black* (`text-[26vw] md:text-[18vw]`, tracking `-0.06em`, line-height `0.82`).
- **Editorial Subheadings:** *Instrument Serif* (`text-3xl md:text-5xl`, italic, font-weight 300).
- **Navigation & Metadata:** *Manrope* (`text-[10px] md:text-[11px]`, font-mono, uppercase, tracking `0.20em` to `0.25em`).
- **Body Prose:** *Manrope* (`text-xs md:text-sm`, font-sans, leading-relaxed, text color `#5B5751`).

All text is rendered via clean semantic HTML/SVG. Zero AI-generated gibberish typography is permitted in any asset.
