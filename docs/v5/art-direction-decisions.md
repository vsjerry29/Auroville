# AUROVILLE V5 — ART DIRECTION DECISIONS & VISUAL TOKEN CONTRACT
**Date:** 23 November 2026

---

## 1. Core Visual Archetype: The Quietly Alive Canvas

The visual identity is defined as **bold and quiet at the same time**:
- Monumental, confident typography (`AURO / VILLE`) in black display weight.
- Fine, hand-drawn graphite and colored-pencil illustrations on ivory archival paper.
- Living background behavior: Faint terrain contour lines that breathe slightly (2–6px) under the pointer and guide the eye along the scroll journey.
- Zero artificial AI tells: No random dot-grids, no concentric target rings, no telemetry coordinates, no repeated all-caps eyebrow badges, and no generic card boxes.

## 3. Spatial & Compositional Rules

1. **One Dominant Hero Per Viewport:**
   - CH01: Monumental `AURO / VILLE` letterforms framing the banyan tree and working potter.
   - CH02: Full-bleed living panorama with the red-earth trail drawing the eye toward the distant golden sphere.
   - CH03: Monumental architectural study evolving continuously across 3 spatial states without slide frames.
   - CH04: Tactile hanging print exhibition that smoothly expands into full-bleed red earth geological terrain.
2. **Continuous Graphic Handoffs:**
   - CH01 → CH02: The Matrimandir elevation outline smoothly unrolls into the edge of the opening paper fold.
   - CH02 → CH03: The red-earth trail straightens into the architectural drafting baseline of the 36m sphere.
   - CH03 → CH04: The curved geodesic disc perimeter unrolls into the hanging linen cord.
   - CH04 → Finale: The hanging red-earth print dissolves its mat margin, expanding into the full landscape with interactive seed sowing.
