## 1. Creative Direction & Aesthetic Contract

1. **Modern Editorial Meets Authentic Illustration:**
   - Bold, massive display typography (`font-display: Archivo Black`) paired with refined, warm editorial serifs (`font-serif: Newsreader / Editorial Italic`).
   - Archival warm ivory paper background (`#F7F4EE`) — completely free of artificial digital dot-grids, neon gradients, or wireframe telemetry.
   - Genuine hand-drawn graphite ink linework and watercolor washes (artisan at wheel, centenary banyan, Matrimandir elevation, winding red-earth trail).
2. **Anti-Slop Cleanliness:**
   - **Zero** generic target rings or concentric crosshairs.
   - **Zero** opaque solid rectangles blocking the landscape or artwork.
   - **Zero** decorative red vector arcs drawn over natural paths.
   - **Zero** permanent white circular hotspot buttons.
   - **Zero** empty vacuum holds where a tiny illustration sits in an isolated white page.

## 3. Technical Implementation Plan

### 3.1 Chapter 01 (`EditorialCover.tsx`)
1. **Layout & Overlap:**
   - Center `AURO / VILLE` with proportional leading (`0.80`) and tracking (`-0.04em`).
   - Anchor `matrimandir-study.webp` at the exact geometric center (`clamp(340px, 44vw, 680px)`), with `z-index` placed between `AURO` and `VILLE` or behind both with high-contrast edge drafting.
   - Left: Potter artisan (`/art/people/potter.webp`) layered cleanly beside `AURO`.
   - Right: Centenary banyan (`/art/botanical/banyan-tree.webp`) framing `AURO` and `VILLE`.
2. **Interactive Behaviors:**
   - **LivingContourField:** Faint graphite terrain contours (2–4px deflection on pointer approach with spring back).
   - **Living Banyan:** Pointer proximity over banyan canopy generates subtle organic displacement (2–4px) on leaf clusters.
   - **Graphite Hatching:** Mouse over Matrimandir reveals delicate ink-hatching overlay layer (`opacity: 0` -> `0.6`).
   - **Artisan Clay Texture:** Pointer near potter reveals subtle throwing swirl lines on the pot.
3. **Scroll Choreography:**
   - Letters translate outward (`translateX(-180px)` and `translateX(180px)`) and clip instead of fading to pale grey.
   - Matrimandir drawing remains fully visible, smoothly expanding (`scale(1.22)`) to prevent any empty dead zone.
   - The drawing's baseline extends as an SVG drafting stroke directly into Chapter 02.

### 3.2 Chapter 02 (`Chapter02PageOpens.tsx` & `PaperFold.tsx`)
1. **Paper Fold Fix:**
   - Replace the wide opaque white panel with a narrow illustrated paper edge (`width: 14–24px` drop shadow).
   - Pre-render the landscape underneath from progress 0.00 so the panorama is immediately revealed as the flap opens.
2. **Asset Layering:**
   - Layer 1: Sky and distant horizon (`panorama-master.webp` base).
   - Layer 2: Forest canopy and Matrimandir dome.
   - Layer 3: Terracotta path and walking figures.
   - Layer 4: Foreground botanicals (`panorama-botanical-fg.webp`).
3. **Interactive Trail & Discoveries:**
   - Remove the thick decorative red vector arc from `StoryPath.tsx`. Replace with an organic line drawing that hugs the actual illustrated path contours.
   - Remove permanent circular hotspot buttons. Replace with subtle illustrated landmark points that reveal integrated sketches on hover/tap.
   - Restrain margin caption to an archival translucent pill in the bottom-left corner with zero occlusion of the pathway.
